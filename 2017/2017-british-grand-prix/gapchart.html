Cada fila es una vuelta, con los pilotos de izquierda a derecha en el orden de esa vuelta. Cada piloto sale de la fila tras su última vuelta.Each row is a lap, with drivers from left to right in that lap's order. It shows the gap to the leader (always in time, never in laps; the leader shows their total time) or, with “see as elapsed time”, each driver's total race time, on-track and before penalties (including the suspension if there was a red flag). Each driver drops off the row after their last lap.Chaque ligne est un tour, avec les pilotes de gauche à droite dans l'ordre de ce tour.
