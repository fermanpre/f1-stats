Cada fila es una vuelta, con los pilotos de izquierda a derecha en el orden de esa vuelta. It shows the gap to the leader (always in time, never in laps; the leader shows their total time) or, with “see as elapsed time”, each driver's total race time, on-track and before penalties (including the suspension if there was a red flag).
