Cada fila es una vuelta, con los pilotos de izquierda a derecha en el orden de esa vuelta. Se muestra la diferencia con el líder (siempre en tiempo, nunca en vueltas; el primero muestra su tiempo total) o, con «ver como tiempo total», el tiempo total de carrera de cada piloto, sobre pista y antes de sanciones (con la suspensión incluida si hubo bandera roja). Cada piloto sale de la fila tras su última vuelta.Each row is a lap, with drivers from left to right in that lap's order. It shows the gap to the leader (always in time, never in laps; the leader shows their total time) or, with “see as elapsed time”, each driver's total race time, on-track and before penalties (including the suspension if there was a red flag). Each driver drops off the row after their last lap.Chaque ligne est un tour, avec les pilotes de gauche à droite dans l'ordre de ce tour. On affiche l'écart avec le leader (toujours en temps, jamais en tours ; le premier affiche son temps total) ou, avec « voir en temps écoulé », le temps de course total de chaque pilote, sur la piste et avant pénalités (suspension incluse en cas de drapeau rouge). order and their gap to the leader (the leader shows their total time). • “see as gaps to leader / see as elapsed time” swaps each cell's gap for the driver's total race time. • “same lap for everyone / same moment for everyone” chooses whether a row is lap N of each driver or the moment the leader completes lap N (lapped drivers show the lap they are on, like L21). • “cumulative / lap times” shows, instead of the accumulated time, how long each driver took on that lap. • A darker grey background means more laps lost, up to five. “Freeze panes” pins the header and the starting-order row
